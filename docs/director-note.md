# Gift boxes online: a first version

*Note for the director of Maison Vauclair · Enzo Chamanier, 21 October 2026 · Demonstration project, fictitious company*

## What I noticed

Almost half of your online sales, about CHF 550,000, happen in the few weeks before Christmas. They mostly come from people who order once a year to give a gift. Yet your gift boxes, your most profitable product, can only be put together in your shops.

This year, Christmas Eve falls on a Thursday, while fresh products only leave from Monday to Wednesday. A customer who orders on Wednesday 23 December receives the box on the 29th, and nobody tells them beforehand.

## What the application does

- Your customers build a gift box online, in French or in German: 4, 6 or 9 products, with the full price shown, shipping included.
- Before ordering, they see the delivery date and the last day to order for Christmas: **Tuesday 22 December 2026**.
- They write a message and the recipient's address. The order appears on a simple team page, sorted by shipping day.
- Your team prints a preparation sheet: the products, the cold or dry zone, the message, the address. No new software to learn.
- Each box can carry a welcome card for the recipient, with a personal code and a QR code. The person who received the gift can become a customer, and you can count how many do.

## What it changes for you

- Your most profitable product can be sold online, right where half of your online sales happen.
- Fewer late deliveries and fewer disappointed customers at Christmas.
- One visitor in five speaks German: the whole journey exists in German.
- Every box sent becomes a chance to win a new customer.

## What it deliberately does not do yet

Payment, customer accounts and the connection to your online shop are left for the next step: they are well-known building blocks, and the aim was first to show you the experience. The application uses no artificial intelligence: no cost per use, and your customers' data stays with you.

## What I propose next

1. Connect the configurator to your online shop, with payment, so that it is live before Christmas.
2. Have it tried by a few loyal customers and by the logistics team.
3. Then address your second goal: an ordering page where restaurants renew their weekly order in one click.

During the presentation, I will also show you what a customer sees on 23 December, when it is already too late for Christmas.
